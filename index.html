AGBref

Global Reference Dataset for Above-Ground Biomass (AGB)

This dataset is part of the AGBref initiative, a global reference dataset for above-ground biomass (AGB) validation derived from national forest inventories (NFIs), permanent research plots, and local airborne LiDAR-based maps.

It contains harmonized AGB estimates across multiple spatial resolutions and epochs, together with associated uncertainties and ecological metadata. It is primarily intended to support the validation of satellite-derived global biomass maps and national carbon accounting.
